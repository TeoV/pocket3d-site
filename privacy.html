Who provides Pocket3D

Pocket3D is provided by Teofil Voivozeanu. For privacy questions or requests, email teofil.voivozeanu@gmail.com.

What the app does

Pocket3D lets you scan objects with the camera and LiDAR on a compatible iPhone, and open 3D model files you select. Capture and model processing run on the device. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, developer-operated cloud storage, or background model-upload service.

Camera and scan data

Camera access is requested when you start scanning. Photo scanning uses Apple’s Object Capture technology with photographs and LiDAR information. Experimental live scanning builds geometry from selected depth observations and camera tracking. Scan photographs, capture/depth information, foreground-selection masks and previews, selection/review records, capture checkpoints, original and polished models, saved settings, and local reconstruction reports stay in app storage. Live projects additionally retain selected depth/confidence buffers, camera calibration and poses, timestamps, capture-volume settings and the generated mesh for replay. Live scanning does not save an RGB video or a photo capture set. You can view imported models without granting camera access. Pocket3D does not request microphone, contacts, location, or general photo-library access.

Imported files and library information

When you choose a file or model folder, Pocket3D copies it and any included dependencies into its local library. It also saves the model name, original filename, import date, source, a random library-item identifier, and your selected units. These records are used to display and manage your library, not to identify you across apps. Original files outside the app remain separate.

What is sent to the developer

The app does not upload your scans, photographs, imported models, measurements, or library records to the developer. There is no integrated advertising or third-party analytics service. The TestFlight and support exceptions described below apply when you use those services.

Files, backups, and Apple services

If you choose a file from iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider may transfer the selected file under its own settings and policy. App data may be included in device or iCloud backups according to your settings. Apple frameworks may download resources needed for their functionality. These operating-system services are separate from a Pocket3D cloud service; Pocket3D does not operate one.

Sharing a model

A model leaves the app when you choose a destination in the system share sheet. The recipient or service receives the file you share, including any information already contained in that file. Check its contents before sharing. The destination’s privacy rules apply. The developer does not automatically receive a copy.

Retention and deletion

Capture projects keep their original photographs, selection masks, review decisions and model attempts locally until you explicitly delete photos or the project. Project names, dates, status and local storage information help you find and reopen captures. Excluding a photo from reconstruction keeps its original. Deleting selected photos removes their originals and associated selections/previews and invalidates capture checkpoints; existing models are kept. Deleting a project removes its capture folder and model attempts. Models already saved in your collection are separate copies and stay there. Deleting a collection model does not delete its capture project. Saved photos from interrupted or failed scans remain available in Capture projects. To remove all Pocket3D data from this device, use Delete App in iOS Settings or delete the app; Offload App can retain its data. Deleting the app does not remove original external files, backups, or copies you previously shared. Manage those with the relevant provider. Earlier beta versions may require deleting the app to remove raw captures that have no project-management interface.

TestFlight beta testing

For TestFlight builds, Apple automatically shares crash logs and usage information with the developer; tester identity and submitted feedback may also be included. We use these reports to troubleshoot and improve Pocket3D, not for advertising. Developer-retained reports are kept only as needed for those purposes. Apple controls its own collection and retention; see TestFlight & Privacy.

Contacting support

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the reported problem, and keep correspondence only as needed for support and applicable obligations. You may request deletion by email. Avoid sending private models or photographs unless you want them included in your request. Opening the email link only opens your mail app; nothing is sent automatically.

This website and external links

The support and privacy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Our site code uses no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, or cookies. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and handles hosting information under its own privacy statement. Opening an external link contacts that site; its privacy rules apply.

Your choices

You can revoke camera permission in iOS Settings and continue using the imported-model viewer. You control which files you import and share. We cannot remotely access or delete files stored only on your device. For privacy questions or requests about support or beta information held by the developer, use the contact below.

Policy updates

If Pocket3D’s privacy practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the app and its store privacy information where applicable.
