Help & support

A little help,
from any angle.

Pocket3D is in beta. These notes cover the current viewer and scanning workflow.

Open a downloaded model

Choose Open a 3D model and select a USDZ, USD, OBJ, or STL file in Files. For a textured OBJ, select its complete folder containing one model and its material/image files. ZIP packages need to be extracted in Files first.

Rotate, zoom, and find your view

Drag with one finger to rotate, pinch to zoom, and drag with two fingers to pan. Fit returns to the perspective view. Front, Side, and Top provide standard angles.

Understand dimensions

Dimensions show the model’s overall width, height, and depth. STL and OBJ coordinates do not specify physical units: choose File units to interpret them. Zoom changes the view while keeping dimensions the same. Scan dimensions are estimates.

Scan an object

Scanning requires a compatible LiDAR-equipped iPhone and camera permission. Keep a rigid object still, use even lighting, and move the phone slowly around it. Additional passes improve coverage. Reflective, transparent, very thin, and deforming objects are difficult to reconstruct.

Keep the app open during reconstruction. Capture projects retain saved photographs and selections so you can review and rebuild after restarting the app. Browse original photos, zoom, and rename projects from Capture projects on the home screen. A new live capture starts a new project; it does not resume an earlier camera session.

Live scanning (experimental beta)

Open Live scan, select a firm horizontal support surface, tap the object’s center, and adjust its box before Start. Keep the object still and move the phone around it. The upper-right inset shows accumulated shaded geometry; Finish saves the current mesh. Capture projects keep selected depth/confidence observations, calibration, poses and settings so you can rebuild without opening the camera.

This mode does not record an RGB video or a photo set. Depth resolution and tracking limit detail. Other objects inside the box may be included, and excluding the table can remove contact geometry. Use a rigid table and inspect holes and scale. A new live scan starts a new coordinate frame; rotating or flipping the object during one scan is unsupported.

Polish a saved scan

After reconstruction, or from a capture project’s Polish saved model button, compare Original and Edited. Smooth appearance changes shading; surface smoothing moves geometry. Begin gently with Preserve edges enabled. Preview again after changing settings, inspect dimension changes, and use the cleaned model to save a separate collection copy. The original reconstruction and photographs remain in the project. Settings are restored when reopening the same source. This beta does not fill holes, cap cuts, or make models watertight for printing.

Share a saved file

Share original in the viewer shares the saved file in its current format. Scans are generated as USDZ. This beta does not yet convert scans to OBJ or STL, or package OBJ textures for sharing.

Delete models and capture data

Deleting a collection model removes its private model copy and entry while keeping its capture project. In Capture projects, select photos to delete their stored originals and selections, or explicitly delete the entire project. Excluding a photo from a build keeps the original. Models already saved in your collection survive project deletion. Earlier beta versions may not have these project controls. To remove all local Pocket3D data, delete the app through iOS Settings; offloading may keep data. Original downloads, backups, and previously shared files remain outside the app.

Save any files you want to keep before deleting the app. Deleting it removes the app’s local library from that device.

Report a problem safely

A description of the issue is usually enough to start. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Pocket3D version/build, model file format if relevant, and the steps that led to the problem.

Only attach screenshots, models, or scan photos you want to share with the developer. You can also use TestFlight feedback when testing a beta build. Read the privacy policy for how support and beta information are handled.
